# Philosophy

## Cultural Foundations
[/cultural foundations]

The cultural foundations branch focuses on the parts of Trillia that are not innately defined by mathematics, but rather by human culture.
These are things like:
- Why Trillia uses English for naming by default
- Why Trillia uses base 10 for numbers by default
- Why Trillia uses ASCII
- Why Trillia uses Standard Technical English for many of its keywords
- Why Trillia uses international place value notation
- Cultural reasoning for operator symbology
- Why is Trillia read from left to right, top to bottom
- Why Trillia uses path notation for member access

*Trillia is a formal language, but it is written by and for humans.
Where purely mathematical choices exist, Trillia chooses them.
Where human convention produces safer, more internationally readable code, Trillia follows convention deliberately.*
